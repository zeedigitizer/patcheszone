Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Visit our full FAQ page or contact us directly.

Is there a minimum order quantity?+
No minimum. Order exactly what you need — even a single patch — though unit price drops as quantity goes up.
How long does production take?+
Standard production is 7–10 business days after artwork approval, plus shipping time to your country.
Do you offer free digitizing?+
Yes. Every order includes free digitizing and a proof for your approval before we start production.
Which backing types are available?+
Iron-on, velcro, sew-on, safety pin and self-adhesive backing are all available and reflected live in the price calculator.
Do you ship internationally?+
Yes, we ship tracked parcels to the USA, Canada, UK, Australia and 80+ other countries worldwide, with free shipping automatically applied on orders of 200+ pcs.
