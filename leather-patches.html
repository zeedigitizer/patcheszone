About Leather Patches

Leather patches bring a premium, tactile finish to hats, bags and branded apparel. We offer genuine and vegan faux-leather options with debossed (pressed), engraved (laser) or full-color printed detail.

  • Genuine or vegan faux-leather
  • Debossed, engraved or full-color print
  • Sew-on or adhesive backing
  • Custom die-cut shapes available
