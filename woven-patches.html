About Woven Patches

Woven patches use a fine thread count woven directly (rather than stitched), which allows for much finer detail, small text and gradients than standard embroidery — while staying flatter and more comfortable against the skin.

  • Finer detail than embroidery
  • Great for small text & gradients
  • Low profile, flat finish
  • Merrow or heat-cut border
