Privacy Policy

How Patches Zone collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: July 23, 2026. This policy is provided as general information for our customers and is not a substitute for independent legal advice.

On This Page

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Your Information 3. Sharing Your Information 4. Cookies & Tracking 5. Data Security 6. Your Rights 7. Data Retention 8. Children's Privacy 9. Changes To This Policy 10. Contact Us

1. Information We Collect

When you use our price calculator, place an order, or contact us, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, order details (patch type, size, quantity, backing, artwork files) and any message content you submit through our forms.

We also automatically collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type and pages visited, used only to keep the site functioning correctly and to understand general usage patterns.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate pricing and process quote requests and orders
  • To communicate with you about your order, artwork proofs and shipping status
  • To respond to contact form submissions and customer support requests
  • To send newsletter updates, if you opt in, which you can unsubscribe from at any time
  • To improve our website, products and customer experience

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted third parties strictly to fulfil your order — for example, shipping carriers for delivery, or payment processors to complete a transaction — and only to the extent necessary for them to perform that service.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This site may use cookies or local storage to remember preferences such as dark mode, and to understand aggregate site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; core site functionality will continue to work without them.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict our use of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We retain order and contact information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, meet legal or accounting requirements, and resolve any disputes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business and personal customers over the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to patcheszone@gmail.com or +92 52 8159000, or by post to House No. 220, Khokhar Town, Sialkot, Punjab 51310, Pakistan.
