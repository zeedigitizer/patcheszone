Iron-On, Velcro or Sew-On? Choosing The Right Backing

Iron-On Backing

The most affordable and beginner-friendly option. A heat-activated adhesive is applied to the back, ready to be pressed onto fabric with a household iron. Best for one-time placement on cotton or poly-blend fabric.

Sew-On Backing

No adhesive at all — just a plain twill back ready for a needle and thread. This is the most durable long-term option and is standard for uniforms and heavy-use apparel.

Velcro (Hook & Loop) Backing

Perfect when patches need to be swapped in and out, like tactical gear, modular uniforms or event badges reused season after season.

Safety Pin & Self-Adhesive

Good for temporary use — trade show badges, one-off events, or anywhere you can't sew or iron. Not recommended for repeated washing.
